## 4) Inspection & incident policy
Inspect before/after every day. Log incidents for:
- Unexpected slips
- Rope edge contact
- Dropped primary devices
- Any “felt wrong” moment

---

## 5) Storage & transport (AZ-specific)
- Textiles stored indoors.
- Metal hardware may live in garage if dry/shaded.
- Any textile exposed to >120°F unintentionally must be inspected.
