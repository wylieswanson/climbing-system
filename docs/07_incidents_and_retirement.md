- Use template: [templates/incident-report-template.md](../templates/incident-report-template.md)

## Retirement principles
- Retire or quarantine any textile with compromised fibers, glazing, melted spots, or failed stitching.
- Retire any hard good with cracks, deformation, or unreliable gate/lock action.
